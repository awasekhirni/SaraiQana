In the nizam era, there used to be SaraiQana along with Turkishbaths for travellers.

"Where every traveller finds a home" , from the caravanserais of the Nizam era to the modern short-term rental revolution.


2-3 Centuries ago, along the trade routes of the Deccan, the Sarai stood as a promise, that no traveller should sleep without shelter, no guest should arrive without welcome. Under the Nizams, the Sarai was more than an inn; it was where cultures met, stories were shared, and strangers became friends.

SaraiQana carries that promise into the digital age, a modern platform connecting travellers with homes, and hosts with guests, reviving the timeless spirit of hospitality with today's technology.




 Core Problem

Short-term rental property managers are bogged down by fragmented, manual operations that scale their workload at the same rate as their business growth, preventing them from operating efficiently and expanding their portfolios.

 Problem Breakdown

 Who faces the problem
- Short-term rental (STR) operators and property managers


Property managers currently juggle disconnected systems and manual processes across:

| Operational Area | Pain Point |
|---|---|
| Reservations & availability | Lack of real-time synchronization across booking channels |
| Guest communication | Repetitive, manual messaging across multiple channels |
| Pricing | No dynamic/intelligent pricing support |
| Cleaning & maintenance | Manual scheduling and coordination; untriaged maintenance requests |
| Payments & owner reporting | Manual financial workflows and statement generation |
| Access management | Manual smart lock/key handling for check-ins |

 Consequences of the problem
- Excessive manual administration — repetitive tasks consume operator time
- Rising costs — operational overhead grows with portfolio size
- Blocked growth — operators cannot scale their portfolios "without increasing their workload at the same rate"
- Operational errors and friction — complex workflows are hard to manage and complete confidently
- Fragmented tooling — reservations, communication, pricing, payments, cleaning, and reporting live in separate systems

 Desired Outcome (the solution's goal)

Build an AI-powered operating system that unifies the entire STR lifecycle into one platform, so operators can:

- Automate manual and repetitive work (event-driven workflows, AI agents)
- Reduce operational costs
- Increase revenue (dynamic pricing, guest upsells)
- Scale their portfolios without a proportional increase in workload
